# BCG vaccine efficacy and latitude

Analysis `latitude` (preregistered); effect measure logRR.

Across 13 studies (13 effects), the pooled logRR was 0.251 (95% CI -0.237 to 0.740). On the ratio scale this is 1.286 (0.789 to 2.095).

Between-study heterogeneity: tau = 0.276 (tau^2 = 0.076), I^2 = 64.2%, Q(11) = 30.733, p = 0.0012.

## Warnings

- **meta-regression-overfitting**: Meta-regression with k = 13 effects and 2 parameters (k/p < 10): overfitting risk.

## Conventions

- Model: random-effects; tau^2 estimator REML; interval wald.
- I^2 and H^2 are Q-based; the prediction interval uses a t distribution on k - 2 degrees of freedom.
