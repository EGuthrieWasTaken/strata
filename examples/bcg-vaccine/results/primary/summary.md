# BCG vaccine and tuberculosis

Analysis `primary` (preregistered); effect measure logRR.

Across 13 studies (13 effects), the pooled logRR was -0.715 (95% CI -1.067 to -0.362). On the ratio scale this is 0.489 (0.344 to 0.696).

Between-study heterogeneity: tau = 0.560 (tau^2 = 0.313), I^2 = 92.1%, Q(12) = 152.233, p = 0.0000.

The 95% prediction interval, where a new study's true effect is expected to fall, is -2.008 to 0.579 (t distribution on k-2 degrees of freedom).

## Warnings

- **high-heterogeneity**: I^2 = 92%: consider whether pooling is appropriate at all.

## Conventions

- Model: random-effects; tau^2 estimator REML; interval wald.
- I^2 and H^2 are Q-based; the prediction interval uses a t distribution on k - 2 degrees of freedom.
