# Proposal: Analysis (M4)

## Why

After M3 a review reaches an analysis-ready dataset but has to leave `strata`
for the statistics, which breaks the provenance chain at exactly the point a
reader cares most about: the pooled estimate. M4 adds a native, validated
meta-analysis engine driven by declarative, versioned analysis files, so
`strata diff` can show a pooled estimate moving when a protocol change moves the
pool. It is the milestone where the project's credibility is established or
lost, so every estimator is validated against `metafor` and published worked
examples before it ships.

Roadmap milestone: M4 ([docs/roadmap.md](../../../docs/roadmap.md)). Design detail: [design.md](design.md).

## What Changes

- Declarative analysis specifications (`analysis/<id>.yaml`) and
  `strata analyze [<id>] [--all] [--check]` writing `analysis/results/<id>/`.
- Effect-size computation and conversion (SMD, MD, OR/RR/RD, ZCOR, HR,
  proportions, pre-post) with flagged conversions.
- Pooling: common-effect and random-effects with eight `tau^2` estimators,
  Wald and Knapp–Hartung intervals, heterogeneity statistics, prediction
  intervals, meta-regression, and dependent-effect handling (aggregate,
  cluster-robust CR2, multilevel/CHE).
- Publication-bias methods, sensitivity analyses, and influence diagnostics.
- Named, recorded guardrails and a reproducibility record (`run.json`).
- The deterministic SVG plot emitter (forest, funnel, Baujat, bubble).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `analysis-specification`: the analysis file, the `strata analyze` run,
  posture (validated or absent), guardrails, and reproducibility.
- `effect-sizes`: effect-size formulae, zero-cell handling, assumed
  correlations, and conversions between reported statistics.
- `pooling-models`: common-effect and random-effects models, `tau^2`
  estimators, confidence and prediction intervals, heterogeneity,
  meta-regression, and dependent effect sizes.
- `bias-and-sensitivity`: publication-bias and small-study methods with their
  guardrail, sensitivity analyses, and influence diagnostics.
- `analysis-plots`: deterministic forest, funnel, bubble, and Baujat plots.

### Modified Capabilities

- `cli`: adds the analysis command group.
- `web-ui`: adds the analysis results screen.

## Impact

- New modules: `strata/stats/` (`escalc`, `models`, `robust`, `bias`,
  `diagnostics`, `engines/native`, `engines/metafor`) with no repository
  knowledge; a deterministic SVG renderer.
- `stats/escalc.py` joins the 100%-branch-coverage modules.
- New CI fixtures generated by a committed R script; the nightly
  `metafor-live` job becomes meaningful.
- No schema-version bump expected: analysis files and results are additive.
